## 7. Human interpretation (trajectory-level review, 2026-09-01)

**All three fixes behave as designed, and no regression of consequence was
found.** The accounting ties out exactly: ground truth 72 (39 L / 33 R),
counted 70 (38 L / 32 R), with the two missing counts fully localised:

* **c31 (-1, right):** car #1632 was absorbed by motorcycle #1619's anchor at
  dacross = 23 px - just inside the 25 px lane gate. A marginal miss;
  tightening DEDUP_ACROSS to ~20 would fix this one instance but would be
  fitting a single video.
* **c32 (-1, left):** truck #1695 was absorbed by truck #1638 - a genuine
  nose-to-tail convoy in the same lane (cy ~315), crossing 8 frames apart. At
  the line this geometry is indistinguishable from a cab/trailer split; it is
  the irreducible hard case for any purely geometric single-line dedup.
* **c35 (net 0, hidden compensation):** the trailer fragment #1796 of truck
  #1707 escaped the merge - a tracking gap pushed its crossing observation to
  frame 818, 19 frames after its anchor (799), beyond the 15-frame window -
  while the genuinely distinct truck #1758 (same lane, crossing 14 frames
  after #1707) was wrongly suppressed. +1 and -1 cancel, so the cluster
  balance is 0. This is the only legitimate merge the new dedup misses, and
  the only distinct vehicle it merges beyond c31/c32.

Fix-specific notes:

* **Fix 1** recovered all 17 line-landing crossings, every one a real
  crossing per the study; 8 of them are correctly re-absorbed as fragments by
  the new dedup, the rest are counted.
* **Fix 2** restored 7 of the 8 vehicles the direction-blind dedup had
  destroyed; the 8th (#2197) is now suppressed by a *same*-direction anchor,
  which is the correct outcome - the study identifies it as a fragment of
  truck #2063.
* **Fix 3** kept every wanted merge except #1796 (window escape above): the
  tanker trio in c03, the double-boxed vehicles in c19/c24/c39/c40, the
  cab/trailer pairs, and - notably - the c28 ID-switch merge (#1429 into
  #1528, 4 frames apart), which the tighter radius was most at risk of
  losing. In c03 the counter now keeps exactly one track per lane
  (#25 box truck / #12 car / #118 tanker) - the correct composition; the
  trajectory review of that cluster also corrected the study's ground truth
  from 2 to 3 vehicles (track #12 is a distinct car in its own lane, not the
  truck cab).

Bottom line: pre-fix AFTER was off by -31 with inverted direction balance;
the fixed counter is off by -2 (-2.8%) with the correct direction balance,
and both residual misses are same-lane near-simultaneous vehicles - not an
artifact of any of the three fixes.
