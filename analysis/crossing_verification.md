The arithmetic ties out exactly:

* 87 net track-crossings = **72 real vehicles + 15 fragment duplicates** (13 rightbound - the long-truck convoys - and 2 leftbound).
* BEFORE counted 70 = 87 minus the **17 crossings missed by the strict `< 0` test** (9 left + 8 right; a center landing exactly on x=640 for one frame makes the product 0 at both transitions, so the crossing is never seen). Verified in the raw CSV for track #717: cx 631 -> 640 -> 647 around frame 250; that was an entire vehicle (event c16) missed by both code versions.
* So per direction: BEFORE left 32 = 41 net - 9 missed; BEFORE right 38 = 46 net - 8 missed; truth left 39 = 41 - 2 dupes; truth right 33 = 46 - 13 dupes.
* The dedup's 8 cross-direction suppressions are all confirmed-real separate vehicles (verified for #558, a leftbound van in the lane at cy~346, absorbed by rightbound car #600 in the lane at cy~406).

### Verdict (human-verified)

**The BEFORE table (70) is far closer to the truth (~72) than the AFTER table (41).** But it is right by coincidence: ~15 fragment double-counts and 17 line-landing misses nearly cancel. The AFTER dedup removes the 15 genuine duplicates *plus roughly 26 real vehicles* (adjacent-lane and closely-following traffic), undercounting by ~40%. Its per-direction skew is also inverted: truth is left-heavy (39L/33R), BEFORE is right-heavy (32L/38R), AFTER keeps the inversion (22L/19R).

### Recommendations (no code changed by this verification)

1. Fix the crossing test so a center landing exactly on the line is not lost (track the last *off-line* side instead of the strict `< 0` product). Worth ~17 counts on this video alone.
2. Make the dedup direction-aware; merging opposite-direction crossings can only ever destroy real counts (8 here).
3. Shrink the dedup radius drastically and make it anisotropic: fragments of one vehicle sit in the same lane (small cross-line offset, here |dcy| < ~25 px), while 80 px spans adjacent lanes. Same-class-or-vehicle-like gating (truck/bus/car confusions) would help.
4. An ID-switch merge (c28) and split fragments (c03, c19, c24, c27, c35, c39, c40) are real - dedup is needed, just much tighter.
